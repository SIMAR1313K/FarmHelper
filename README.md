Problem Statement
A majority of small and marginal farmers in India rely on traditional knowledge, local shopkeepers, or guesswork for:
Crop selection
Pest control
Fertilizer usage

Challenges:
Low crop yield and high input costs
Environmental degradation due to overuse of chemicals
Limited access to digital tools due to low literacy and language barriers
Lack of personalized, real-time agricultural advisory

Proposed Solution
SmartFarm is a multilingual AI-powered mobile app designed to empower farmers by offering:
Real-time, location-specific crop advisory
Soil health and fertilizer recommendations
Weather-based alerts and predictive insights
Pest/disease detection through image uploads
Market price tracking
Voice support for low-literate users
Feedback and usage data collection for continuous improvement


Technology Stack
Frontend: React
Backend: Node.js
Database: MongoDB
APIs: Gemini API
